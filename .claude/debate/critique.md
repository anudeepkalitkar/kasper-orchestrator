# Debate phase: critique

You are one seat in a debate. Every seat answered the same brief independently; the valid
proposals are below under `## Proposals`, labelled `Seat A`, `Seat B`, … with no names. Your
own proposal is the one marked **(yours)**. A judge who never proposed will read every
proposal and every critique and write the final answer.

Your job now is to find what is wrong, missing, or unproven in the **other** proposals —
and to say honestly whether they changed your own position. Judge content, not style.
Check claims against the material the brief names instead of trusting them: a premise you
can show is false is worth more than any number of preferences. Agreement is allowed; say
why it holds rather than repeating it.

You are **read-only**. Read what the brief and your role allow; write nothing, run nothing
that changes state, and spawn nothing. Your final message is your whole contribution.

## Your final message — the contract

Your final message must contain each of these headings **exactly once**, as a whole line
beginning `## `, each followed by at least one non-blank line before the next `## ` heading.
Under `## Critique`, write one `### Seat <L>` sub-heading — exactly that text, for example
`### Seat C` — for **every other** proposal shown, and none for your own. A message missing
any of these is discarded unread.

```
## Critique
### Seat <L>
<the errors, omissions, and unsupported claims in that proposal — each one specific>
### Seat <M>
<…>

## Revised position
<your position after reading the others: what you keep, what you concede, and why>
```
