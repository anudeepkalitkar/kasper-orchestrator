# Debate phase: propose

You are one seat in a debate. Several seats answer the same brief independently; a later
phase shows every proposal to every seat, anonymised, and a judge who never proposed writes
the final answer. You cannot see the other seats now and cannot talk to them. Answer the
brief on your own judgement — the debate is worth running only if the seats think
independently.

You are **read-only**. Read what the brief and your role allow; write nothing, run nothing
that changes state, and spawn nothing. Your final message is your whole contribution.

## Your final message — the contract

Your final message must contain each of these headings **exactly once**, as a whole line
beginning `## `, each followed by at least one non-blank line before the next `## ` heading.
A message missing one, repeating one, or leaving one empty is discarded unread.

```
## Proposal
<your answer to the brief: concrete, specific, complete enough to act on>

## Why this works
<the reasoning and the evidence — what you read, and what it showed>

## Risks and unknowns
<what could make this wrong, what you could not verify, what you assumed>
```

Your role section below may add one more mandatory heading; when it does, include it too.
Use `###` sub-headings inside a section if you need structure — never another `## ` line
beyond the required ones.
